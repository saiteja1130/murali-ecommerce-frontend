# Product Context (murali-ecommerce-frontend)

## 1. Brand Identity & Aesthetics
- **Brand Name**: SUMILUX Luxury & Contemporary Boutique
- **Palette**: Deep Obsidian (`#1D241C`), Warm Gold (`#C69E58`), Botanical Sage (`#506040`), Ivory (`#FAF8F5`).
- **Typography**: Playfair Display (Serif) + Plus Jakarta Sans (Sans-serif).

## 2. User Flows
1. **Catalog Browsing**: Real-time filtering, responsive grid/list switcher, infinite scrolling.
2. **Product Details**: Multi-angle image viewing, size/color variant selection, collapsible info accordions.
3. **Quick Add**: 1-click cart addition from product cards with bottom-right toast notification.
4. **Checkout**: Address selection, payment method selection, order confirmation.
